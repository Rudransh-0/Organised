# Design System: Organised (AI-Powered To-Do List)

## 1. Visual Theme & Atmosphere

Organised is designed as a serene, tactile sanctuary from cognitive overwhelm. Traditional to-do apps provoke anxiety with rigid folders, endless form fields, and cluttered metadata dials. In contrast, Organised feels like an open sheet of heavyweight cotton paper in a well-lit architecture studio — airy, deliberate, and calming.

- **Density: 4 / 10 ("Daily App Balanced" with "Art Gallery Airy" breathing room)**  
  Generous internal padding (`2rem` / `32px` on desktop containers) ensures thoughts never feel crowded. Elements maintain clear spatial separation without feeling sparse.
- **Variance: 6 / 10 ("Offset Asymmetric Structure")**  
  An asymmetric rhythm separates the fluid, prominent Brain-Dump Input Canvas from the structured 4-column Urgency Board below.
- **Motion: 6 / 10 ("Fluid Spring Physics & Micro-Tactile Feedback")**  
  Interactions feel physical and weighted — subtle active button depressions, smooth card transitions, and waterfall reveals that give the user immediate visual confirmation that their mental clutter has been resolved.

---

## 2. Color Palette & Roles

The palette is anchored in warm architectural neutrals with a singular earthy accent. The generic "AI Neon Purple / Cyber Blue" palette is strictly banned.

### Primary Surfaces & Neutrals
- **Canvas Mist (`#FBFBFA`)** — Primary ambient background. Subtle warm off-white that reduces eye fatigue compared to harsh sterile whites.
- **Paper Surface (`#FFFFFF`)** — Elevated card containers, brain-dump input box, and dialog sheets.
- **Charcoal Ink (`#16171A`)** — Primary text and high-contrast structural labels (Zinc-950 depth). Never pure black (`#000000`).
- **Muted Slate (`#64748B`)** — Secondary text, column descriptions, and helper hints.
- **Whisper Border (`rgba(228, 228, 231, 0.75)`)** — 1px crisp structural borders on cards and inputs.

### System Accent (Max 1 Primary Accent)
- **Terracotta Ember (`#C25936`)** — Singular primary accent (Saturation: 57%). Used exclusively for primary interactive focal points: the "Organize" CTA button, active microphone recording pulse, input focus rings, and pinned state badges.

### Calibrated Category Tokens (Desaturated Organic Pips)
Categories generated by the AI are represented via subtle 6px left-border accents or 8px circular pips on task cards — never garish card backgrounds:
- **Focus & Work:** *Slate Indigo* (`#475569`)
- **Personal & Living:** *Forest Celadon* (`#2E6F54`)
- **Urgent & Vital:** *Warm Brick* (`#A8422B`)
- **Errands & Shopping:** *Raw Ochre* (`#8C6D3B`)
- **Health & Wellness:** *Dusty Sage* (`#5A786B`)

## 3. Typography Rules

Typography emphasizes clarity and weight-driven hierarchy rather than gigantic font sizes.

- **Display / Headlines:** `Cabinet Grotesk` (Fallback: `Outfit`, `system-ui`)  
  Track-tight (`letter-spacing: -0.03em`), confident, warm grotesque letterforms. High-contrast titles that convey calm authority.
- **Body & Prompts:** `Satoshi` (Fallback: `sans-serif`)  
  Relaxed leading (`line-height: 1.6`), max 65 characters per line (`max-w-[65ch]`). Highly readable at standard `1rem` (16px) body scale.
- **Monospace & Metadata:** `JetBrains Mono` (Fallback: `ui-monospace`)  
  Used for all high-density numbers: column task counters (`[03]`), deterministic due dates (`Tomorrow, 18:00`), pin indicators, and keyboard hints.
- **Banned:** `Inter` is banned for creative UI surfaces. Generic system serifs (`Times New Roman`, `Georgia`) are strictly banned.

---

## 4. Component Stylings

### A. The Brain-Dump Input Canvas
- **Container:** Elevated `Paper Surface` (`#FFFFFF`) with a subtle `Whisper Border` and diffused ambient drop shadow (`0 8px 30px rgba(0,0,0,0.04)`). Generously rounded (`1.5rem` / `24px`).
- **Textarea:** Seamless, borderless auto-expanding area. Placeholder text in `Muted Slate`: *"Speak or type whatever is on your mind... laundry tonight, email Sarah by Friday, pay electric bill"*.
- **Microphone Button:** Circular tactile button (`48px x 48px`). In active recording state: subtle breathing ripple ring using `Terracotta Ember` at 20% opacity.
- **"Organize" Button:** Solid `Terracotta Ember` (`#C25936`), crisp white text, tactile `-1px` vertical translation on `:active`.

### B. Dynamic Urgency Board (4 Fixed Columns)
The board organizes tasks across four structural columns: **Urgent**, **Upcoming**, **Backlog**, and **Undefined**.
- **Column Headers:** Monospace uppercase labels (e.g., `URGENT · 03`) with a muted counter badge in `JetBrains Mono`.
- **Column Background:** Transparent with very faint 1px border dividers (`rgba(228, 228, 231, 0.4)`).
- **Target Drop State:** When a card is long-pressed/right-clicked, all four columns illuminate with a subtle dashed highlight (`#C25936` at 15% opacity) indicating they are one-tap move targets.

### C. Task Cards
- **Geometry:** Rounded corners (`1.25rem` / `20px`), clean `Paper Surface` fill, 1px `Whisper Border`.
- **Shadow:** Subtle resting depth (`0 2px 8px rgba(0,0,0,0.02)`), rising to (`0 8px 24px rgba(0,0,0,0.06)`) on hover/drag.
- **Category Indicator:** 3px vertical accent bar on the left edge tinted to the task's assigned category token.
- **Pinned Indicator:** When manually moved by the user, a small `14px` geometric pin icon appears beside the date in `Terracotta Ember`, signifying that auto-recomputation is locked.
- **Contextual Actions (On Long-Press / Right-Click):**
  - **Quick Complete Checkmark:** Circular emerald ring (`#2E6F54`) for instant one-tap completion.
  - **More ("..."):** Opens the inline task edit drawer (title, category, date picker with explicit *"Clear date"*).

### D. System Feedback & Alerts
- **Thinking / Processing Loader:** Segmented undulating wave shimmer across the input canvas matching the layout dimensions. Circular spinners are banned.
- **High-Traffic Graceful Fallback:** Friendly, calm banner: *"We're experiencing heavy traffic right now — please wait a moment and try again."* Preserves the raw text completely.
- **Undo Toast:** Low-profile pill floating at bottom center: *"Task completed · [Undo]"* for a 5-second recovery window.

## 5. Layout Principles

- **Grid Architecture:** Responsive 4-column CSS grid on desktop (`minmax(260px, 1fr)`). Max-width container capped at `1360px` centered with auto margins.
- **Mobile-First Responsive Strategy (< 768px):**
  - Columns collapse to a single active column view paired with a tactile horizontal tab switcher (`Urgent`, `Upcoming`, `Backlog`, `Undefined`).
  - The Brain-Dump input remains permanently accessible at top or bottom sticky bar.
  - All touch targets strictly maintain a minimum `48px` tappable area.
- **Full Viewport Safety:** Use `min-h-[100dvh]` to prevent catastrophic layout jumps from mobile browser navigation address bars. Never use `h-screen`.
- **Strict Spatial Zones:** Zero overlapping elements. No absolute-positioned content collisions.

---

## 6. Motion & Interaction

- **Physics Engine:** Spring physics curve default (`stiffness: 120, damping: 20`). No linear transitions (`cubic-bezier` with crisp decel).
- **Waterfall Ingestion:** When tasks are parsed by the AI, cards mount sequentially with a `40ms` stagger delay (`translateY(8px) → translateY(0)`, `opacity 0 → 1`).
- **Completion Fade:** Tapping complete triggers an immediate card scale (`scale(0.97)`), green flash, and smooth vertical collapse (`max-height: 0`, `opacity: 0`).
- **Hardware Acceleration:** Animations strictly constrained to `transform` and `opacity`. Animating layout properties (`width`, `height`, `top`, `left`) is banned.

---

## 7. Anti-Patterns (Strictly Banned)

1. **No Emojis:** Do not use emojis in UI headers, buttons, column titles, or generated task categories. Use semantic geometric SVG icons only.
2. **No Generic Fonts:** `Inter`, `Times New Roman`, `Georgia`, and generic system fallbacks are banned.
3. **No Pure Black:** `#000000` is banned. Use `Charcoal Ink` (`#16171A`).
4. **No Neon / Cyber Glows:** No purple button glows, no pulsing saturated rings, no cyberpunk gradients.
5. **No Centered Marketing Heros:** The input area is a functional workstation, not a marketing landing page. No "Scroll to explore" text, no bouncing chevrons.
6. **No AI Copywriting Clichés:** Never use "Unleash", "Seamless", "Elevate your workflow", "Next-Gen AI Magic".
7. **No 3-Equal Feature Card Rows:** The task board is strictly 4 urgency-driven columns with purposeful state handling.
8. **No Silent Quality Degradation:** Never display a degraded or partial task result if AI extraction fails. Provide an honest, calm retry prompt.
